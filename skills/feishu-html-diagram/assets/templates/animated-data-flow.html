Continuous learning loop

Signals become better decisions

A closed loop captures events, interprets context, acts through policy, and returns measured outcomes as the next signal.

  1. 1 · CaptureNormalize the signal without losing its source.
  2. 2 · InterpretAdd context and confidence before choosing.
  3. 3 · DecideAct inside explicit policy and permission.
  4. 4 · LearnReturn measured outcomes to the next cycle.
