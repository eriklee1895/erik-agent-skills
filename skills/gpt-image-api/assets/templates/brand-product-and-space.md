# Brand, product, and space templates

These templates support commercial concepting. Verify claims, names, dates, labels, and
brand assets before generation. A raster logo or layout concept is not a production vector
or a finished brand guideline.

## Product hero

**Use when:** A single product needs a catalog, storefront, campaign, or landing-page hero.

**Fixed requirements:** Lock product identity, geometry, label copy, material, required
view, delivery slot, aspect ratio, and any reserved layout area.

**Creative latitude:** Choose set design, plinth, camera nuance, light, palette, styling,
and surface treatment when the brand has not fixed them.

```text
Deliverable: [placement and ratio] hero image for [brand/product context].
Primary request: one [product], with [defining geometry, material, and label] clearly visible.
Composition: [required view], product in [frame position], [reserved area] left usable for downstream layout.
Lighting and color: [brand direction or agent-selected coherent setup], realistic contact shadow and reflections.
Material behavior: [matte/gloss/translucent/fabric] with [critical texture and edge behavior].
Text (exact): render "[APPROVED LABEL]" exactly once at [location], [type requirement].
Avoid: duplicate product, changed geometry, invented accessories, extra text, fake logo, or watermark.
```

**Visual QA:** Compare geometry and every label character, then inspect silhouette,
material, contact, reflections, crop, negative space, color, and unwanted duplicates.

**Evidence/status:** Official product and exact-text practices plus the existing locally reviewed product recipe.

## Reusable logo or brand identity package

**Use when:** Explore an original logo concept together with a compact identity system.

**Fixed requirements:** Lock approved brand name, positioning, audience, required symbol
idea, applications, originality, and any forbidden visual territory.

**Creative latitude:** Invent symbol construction, typography character, palette, spacing,
support graphics, and touchpoint expression consistent with the brief.

```text
Deliverable: one coherent brand identity presentation for "[BRAND NAME]", [business and positioning].
Core idea: [approved concept, values, audience, and desired personality].
Logo: original symbol/wordmark with strong silhouette, balanced negative space, and clear small-size behavior.
System: primary/secondary colors, typography character, spacing logic, graphic motif, and [required applications].
Presentation: organized identity board with clear hierarchy; distinguish core marks from examples.
Text (exact): "[BRAND NAME]" and only [approved supporting labels].
Avoid: copied trademarks, generic symbol clichés, random mockups, inconsistent marks, extra slogans, or watermark.
```

**Visual QA:** Check name spelling, originality, mark consistency, small-scale legibility,
palette reuse, typography hierarchy, spacing, and whether touchpoints share one system.

**Evidence/status:** Official reusable-logo guidance combined with a selective community [brand identity package](https://github.com/freestylefly/awesome-gpt-image-2/blob/main/docs/templates.md); final logos require vector reconstruction and clearance.

## Brand touchpoint or campaign board

**Use when:** Show one established identity across packaging, web, social, environmental,
or campaign contexts.

**Fixed requirements:** Lock approved logo, name, palette, typography, campaign copy,
required touchpoints, product identity, and deliverable count.

**Creative latitude:** Design composition, mockup angles, scene treatment, hierarchy, and
supporting graphic rhythm while applying the supplied identity consistently.

```text
Images 1..N: approved logo, product, packaging, and identity references with explicit roles.
Deliverable: one [ratio] brand touchpoint board showing exactly [count] applications: [list].
Apply consistently: approved logo geometry, brand name, palette, typography character, graphic motif, and campaign line.
Composition: clear master hierarchy with separated but related touchpoint views.
Text (exact): [approved copy and occurrence rules].
Avoid: logo redesign, invented slogans, changed product labels, inconsistent colors, unrelated mockups, or watermark.
```

**Visual QA:** Compare logo/product references, count touchpoints, verify all copy, palette,
type consistency, application realism, hierarchy, and accidental identity variations.

**Evidence/status:** Community brand-touchpoint pattern rewritten as a reference-guided 2.5 composition template; not a substitute for a deterministic brand guideline.

## Architecture or interior visualization

**Use when:** Visualize a building, room, landscape, or spatial concept from text, plans,
sketches, or an existing photo.

**Fixed requirements:** Lock program, dimensions or proportions, openings, circulation,
viewpoint, protected structure, accessibility, and any supplied plan/sketch geometry.

**Creative latitude:** Choose materials, furnishings, planting, atmosphere, daylight,
practical lights, and lived-in detail consistent with the design intent.

```text
[Image 1: plan/sketch/existing space and geometry reference, if supplied.]
Deliverable: [exterior/interior/landscape] visualization of [project and program].
Spatial lock: preserve [layout, dimensions/proportions, openings, structural rhythm, circulation, viewpoint].
Design intent: [experience, occupancy, style direction, and required functional zones].
Materials and detail: [approved materials or agent-selected plausible palette], realistic junctions and scale cues.
Lighting: [time/daylight direction/practical lights] with physically coherent shadows and reflections.
Avoid: changed plan geometry, impossible openings, blocked circulation, floating furniture, inconsistent scale, extra text, or watermark.
```

**Visual QA:** Compare plan/sketch geometry, then inspect circulation, openings, scale,
material junctions, furniture contact, light direction, reflections, and impossible structure.

**Evidence/status:** Community architecture category plus official sketch-preservation principles; spatial feasibility still requires architectural review.

## Concept product development breakdown board

**Use when:** Show how a physical inspiration evolves into a product through analysis,
iteration, structure, ergonomics, materials, and a final concept.

**Fixed requirements:** Lock inspiration source, product type, user, functional goals,
required stages, material constraints, dimensions/specs if known, and panel count.

**Creative latitude:** Invent formal transformations, sketch language, exploded-view style,
annotation system, palette, and board composition while keeping the reasoning coherent.

```text
Deliverable: [ratio] product-development breakdown board for a [PRODUCT TYPE] inspired by [SOURCE].
Structure: exactly [count] ordered regions showing observation, form extraction, [3-5] iterations, ergonomic testing, structural integration, materials, and final concept.
Each region: short exact heading, relevant sketch/render, and one concise verified annotation.
Final product: [functional requirements, user, dimensions/specs, materials, and distinctive features].
Visual language: industrial-design development board with clear progression, construction lines, exploded layers, and disciplined hierarchy.
Avoid: a single beauty render without process, arbitrary evolution, impossible structure, fake specifications, long paragraphs, or watermark.
```

**Visual QA:** Count stages, verify progression from source to form, ergonomics, structure,
material logic, annotation accuracy, final-product continuity, and absence of invented specs.

**Evidence/status:** Selective adaptation of the community [concept product breakdown](https://github.com/freestylefly/awesome-gpt-image-2/blob/main/docs/templates.md); engineering claims require independent validation.
